Important: Correct Auto Open & Close Instructions

Proper operation helps protect the automatic mechanism and keeps your umbrella working smoothly.

Before Opening

  1. 1Unfasten the strap.
  2. 2Gently shake the umbrella to loosen the canopy and ribs.
  3. 3Press the button to open.

To Close

  1. 1Press the button once to close the canopy.
  2. 2Then use both hands—one on the handle and one near the top of the shaft—to push the shaft together in one quick, controlled motion until it locks.

Simple habits that help

Care Tips

Dry Fully

Allow the umbrella to air-dry before long-term storage.

Loosen Before Opening

Gently loosen the canopy before pressing the automatic open button.

Store Dry

Keep the umbrella in a cool, dry place. Never store it while wet.

Avoid Strong Wind

Avoid using the umbrella in severe wind or conditions beyond its intended use.
