Privacy Notice

How this website uses analytics and support information.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Scope

This notice applies to the Ward Rain&Sun website, including the Warranty Help & Care page. The current support page is intended for Amazon.com customers in the United States. It does not contain an online warranty form, account system, file upload, or customer-record database.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used and to improve page performance and support content. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies and similar identifiers. Information collected by default may include:

  • pages viewed, referral information, and general interaction or session data;
  • browser, device, operating-system, and screen information; and
  • approximate geographic information derived by Google from technical data.

Google Signals and advertising-personalization signals are disabled in our site configuration. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, Amazon order numbers, or product photos to Google Analytics.

Learn more about data collected by Google Analytics and Google’s privacy practices.

Information you choose to email

If you contact support@wardrainsun.com, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your question or product-support request. The website itself does not collect the contents of your email.

Amazon and other external links

Links to Amazon Your Orders, Google, or other websites take you to services governed by their own privacy notices. We do not control those third-party services.

Your choices

You can limit or remove cookies through your browser settings. Google also offers a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking analytics may reduce our ability to measure site performance but does not prevent you from reading the support information.

Contact

For questions about this notice, email support@wardrainsun.com.
