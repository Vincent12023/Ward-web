Care & safe use

Simple habits for everyday use

These general safety and care notes apply before any model-specific operating instructions are published.

1

Dry it fully

After wet use, open the umbrella in a ventilated place and let it dry completely.

2

Store it dry

Close and store the umbrella only after the canopy and frame are dry.

3

Avoid strong wind

Conditions can change quickly. Do not rely on an umbrella in severe or dangerous wind.

4

Do not force it

If the mechanism feels abnormal, stop forcing it and seek product support.
