Product Care

Useful Tips for a Longer Umbrella Life

Dry Before Storing

Allow the umbrella to air-dry before long-term storage.

Use the Button as Intended

Use the automatic open / close button and reset the shaft correctly.

Loosen the Canopy First

After unfastening the strap, gently loosen the canopy before opening.

Use Extra Care in Severe Weather

Avoid using the umbrella in conditions beyond its intended use.
