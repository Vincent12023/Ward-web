Ward Rain&Sun Lifetime Limited Warranty

This Preview outlines the intended consumer-facing structure. Final legal language and several policy details have not been approved.

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: This is a working Preview, not the final warranty policy. It must not be used as production legal language.

Overview

Ward Rain&Sun provides a Lifetime Warranty to consumers. The formal name of this coverage is the Ward Rain&Sun Lifetime Limited Warranty. Coverage is subject to the eligibility requirements, exclusions, and remedy terms in the final approved policy.

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED — Define “Lifetime” explicitly before production launch. Do not assume whether it means the original purchaser's ownership period, the product's useful lifetime, or another legally reviewed definition.

Who Is Covered

The intended covered person is the original purchaser. Proof of purchase may be required when requesting warranty support.

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED — Confirm transferability, territorial scope, acceptable proof of purchase, and any jurisdiction-specific requirements.

What Is Covered

The core coverage principle is: Eligible defects in materials and workmanship under normal and intended use.

Subject to the final policy and claim assessment, eligible defects may include:

  • Frame or rib manufacturing defects
  • Shaft defects
  • Automatic mechanism defects
  • Handle attachment defects
  • Stitching or assembly defects
  • Other eligible material or manufacturing defects

Not every instance of product damage is automatically covered. Eligibility depends on the facts of the support request and the final approved warranty terms.

What Is Not Covered

Subject to final legal approval, exclusions are expected to include:

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Cosmetic damage from normal use
  • Accidental damage
  • Misuse or improper operation
  • Improper storage or maintenance
  • Loss or theft
  • Unauthorized repair or modification
  • Damage caused by severe weather
  • Damage caused by use beyond intended conditions
LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED — Confirm the precise scope and wording of every exclusion before production launch.

Warranty Remedy

If a claim is confirmed to be within warranty coverage, Ward Rain&Sun may choose an appropriate remedy based on the circumstances, which may include:

  • Repair
  • Eligible component replacement
  • Same-product replacement
  • Comparable-product replacement

The appropriate remedy depends on product condition, eligibility, and availability under the final approved policy.

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED — Confirm remedy discretion, availability standards, shipping responsibility, and any limits before production launch.

How to Obtain Warranty Support

To help Ward Rain&Sun understand and evaluate a product issue, please be ready to provide:

  • Proof of purchase or Amazon Order ID
  • A clear full-product photo
  • A photo showing the issue
  • A brief description of the problem

Start through the Product Support & Warranty Claim Preview or contact support@wardrainsun.com.
