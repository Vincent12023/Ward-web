Item 2 · Important operating guide

Important: Correct Auto Open & Close Instructions

Proper opening and closing helps protect the automatic mechanism and keeps your umbrella working smoothly.

Before Opening

1
Unfasten the strap.
2
Gently shake the umbrella to loosen the canopy and ribs.
3
Press the button to open.

To Close

1
Press the button once.The canopy will automatically close.
2
Hold the handle with one hand and place your other hand near the upper part of the shaft.
3
Push the shaft firmly together in one continuous motion until it locks.

Product Care

Useful Tips for a Longer Umbrella Life

Dry Before Storing

Allow the umbrella to air-dry before long-term storage.

Don't Force the Mechanism

Always use the automatic open / close button as intended and reset the shaft correctly.

Loosen the Canopy Before Opening

After unfastening the strap, gently loosen the canopy before pressing the open button.

Use Extra Care in Severe Weather

Avoid using the umbrella in conditions beyond its intended use.
