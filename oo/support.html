Frequently asked questions

For purchases made on Amazon.com, Amazon Your Orders is the primary place to start. Ward Rain&Sun support is available for product-care and included-card questions.

Warranty help

Do I need to complete an online form before requesting help?

No online form is required on this website. Please keep your proof of purchase. For an Amazon.com order, begin in Amazon Your Orders to view the support options associated with your purchase.

What should I do if the wording on my included card is confusing?

You are in the right place. The card directs customers to Ward Rain&Sun support, but this site does not require an online setup step. For questions about the card, email support@wardrainsun.com.

Does this page define the coverage or remedy for my product?

No. This page is a support guide and does not create or replace formal warranty terms. Start with Amazon Your Orders for your purchase, or contact Ward Rain&Sun if you need help understanding the included card.

Product issues

My umbrella is damaged or is not working normally. Where do I start?

Stop using or forcing the product if doing so could be unsafe. For an Amazon.com purchase, open Amazon Your Orders first. If your question is about product care or the included card, you can also contact Ward Rain&Sun support.

What information should I include in my first email?

A brief description of your question is enough to begin. The email link on this site adds only a general subject line. Do not send sensitive personal information. Additional details can be requested if they are needed to address your question.

Care & safe use

How should I dry and store my umbrella?

After wet use, open the umbrella in a ventilated place and let it dry completely. Store it only after the canopy and frame are dry.

What should I do in strong wind?

Avoid using an umbrella in severe or dangerous wind. Weather conditions can change quickly, so prioritize personal safety and seek shelter when needed.

The mechanism feels unusual. Should I keep trying it?

No. Stop forcing the mechanism and seek product support. Model-specific operating steps will be published only after they have been verified against the physical product.

Amazon orders

How do I find my order or purchase options?

Sign in to Amazon.com and open Your Orders. Select the relevant purchase to see the order details and options Amazon provides.

I want to return or exchange my umbrella. What should I do?

Use Amazon Your Orders for standard return or exchange options on a purchase made through Amazon.com. The options displayed there are tied to your order.
